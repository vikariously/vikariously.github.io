A bright, botanical highball that pairs the herbaceous notes of St. George Botanivore Gin with crisp lemon juice and floral elderflower tonic. It is effortless to build in the glass and makes a refreshing warm-weather sipper.

**Yield:** 1 cocktail

## Ingredients

* 2 oz. St. George Botanivore Gin
* 1 oz. fresh squeezed lemon juice
* 5 oz. Fever-Tree Elderflower Tonic

## Directions

1. Add the gin and lemon juice to an ice-filled glass.

1. Top with the elderflower tonic and stir gently to combine.
