Miss Jones Baking Co. pumpkin bread mix, upgraded with real pumpkin purée and baked as mini-loaves. You get the soft, tender crumb of a full loaf in about half the bake time. The purée makes the batter thick, dense, and extra moist.

**Yield:** 3–4 standard mini-loaves (depends on your pan's cavity size)

## Ingredients

* 1 pouch Miss Jones Baking Co. pumpkin bread mix (dry mix only; the topping and icing packets are used below)
* 2 eggs
* ⅓ cup oil
* ¼ cup water
* ¾ cup pumpkin purée
* 1 packet cinnamon streusel topping (included in the box)
* 1 packet cream cheese icing (included in the box)
* Cooking spray or oil, for the pan

## Directions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease the mini-loaf pan thoroughly with cooking spray or oil.

1. Mix the dry mix pouch, eggs, oil, water, and pumpkin purée into a batter.

1. Divide the batter evenly among the mini-loaf cavities, filling each ⅔ to ¾ full.

1. Sprinkle the cinnamon streusel topping evenly over all the loaves.

1. Center the pan on the middle rack and bake at 350°F for 30–35 minutes. At the 20-minute mark, rotate the pan 180° so oven hot spots don't leave some loaves over-baked and others under-baked.

1. Start checking at 28 minutes: insert a toothpick into the center of a *middle* loaf. Those cavities get the least direct heat, so if they're done, the whole pan is done. It should come out clean or with a few moist crumbs, but no wet batter.

1. Cool in the pan on a wire rack for 10 minutes, then turn the loaves out onto the rack to cool completely.

1. Once fully cool, drizzle the cream cheese icing over the tops.
