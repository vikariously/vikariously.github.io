A herbal simple syrup that gives our [Purple Reign](/recipes/purple-reign) its savory edge. Keep it in the fridge for cocktails, lemonade or iced tea.

## Ingredients

* 1 cup water
* 1 cup granulated white sugar
* 8–10 sprigs fresh thyme

## Directions

1. Remove any woody stems from the thyme. Use the rest as shorter stems or just leaves, as you prefer.

1. Add the water, sugar and thyme to a small pan over medium heat, stirring regularly, until the sugar dissolves and the mixture is just starting to simmer.

1. Remove the pan from the heat and let the thyme steep for 15–20 minutes as the syrup cools.

1. Pour the syrup through a fine strainer to hold back the thyme. Store it in a sealed bottle or jar in the fridge.
