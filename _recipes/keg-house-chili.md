Our house chili: a big, cozy batch of ground turkey, three kinds of beans, and just enough brown sugar to round out the heat. It comes together low-and-slow in the crock pot, or fast in the Instant Pot when you don't feel like waiting. Make the full batch — it freezes beautifully for future lunches and dinners.

## Ingredients

* 1 large yellow onion, finely diced
* 1 lb ground turkey
* 4 cans petite diced tomatoes
* 3 cans pinto beans
* 2 cans kidney beans
* 1 can black beans
* ½ can tomato paste
* 1-2 poblanos, diced (optional; treat like the onions)
* ⅓ cup brown sugar
* 3 Tbsp chili powder
* 1 tsp cumin
* 1 tsp oregano
* 6-10 dashes hot sauce (habanero)
* Garlic powder, salt & pepper, to taste
* Red pepper flakes, to taste


## Directions

1. Finely dice the onion and begin to sauté it in a large pan (add the poblanos here too, if using).

1. Lay the ground turkey flat in a pan and cover with garlic powder, salt, pepper, cumin, oregano, red pepper flakes, chili powder, and several dashes of hot sauce. Break it up and brown.

1. Open all the cans, drain them, and add all the canned goods to the crock pot. Add the sautéed onion and browned turkey, then stir in the brown sugar, chili powder, cumin, and oregano.

1. Further season the pot to taste with salt, pepper, garlic powder, red pepper flakes, and hot sauce. Let it simmer in the crock pot until everything is hot through and the flavors have melded (a few hours on low).

##### Instant Pot option:

* Season a little lighter: ~1 Tbsp garlic powder, ½ Tbsp salt, ½ Tbsp pepper, no red pepper flakes (sub ~1-2 tsp hot sauce), and skip the tomato paste.
* Sauté the turkey to brown a little, then add the onions and sauté until translucent (~5 min).
* Add everything else, pressure cook on High for 5 minutes, then slow release for 10+ minutes.

### Notes

* This makes a big batch — perfect for feeding a crowd or stashing away.
* **Freezing:** Chili freezes great. Portion it out and thaw overnight for easy future meals.
