Our go-to house gin and tonic, built around floral Nolet's Silver Gin, a generous full ounce of fresh lime juice, and crisp Fever-Tree Refreshingly Light tonic. It's bright, tart, and effortlessly refreshing on a warm evening.

**Yield:** 1 cocktail

## Ingredients

* 2 oz. Nolet's Silver Gin
* 1 oz. fresh squeezed lime juice
* 5 oz. Fever-Tree Refreshingly Light Tonic
* 1 lime wheel (optional), for garnish

## Directions

1. Combine the gin and fresh lime juice in a glass filled with ice.

1. Top with the tonic water and stir gently to combine.

1. Garnish with a lime wheel, if desired, and serve.
