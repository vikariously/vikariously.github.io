A warm, autumnal whiskey cocktail that pairs spicy high-rye bourbon with sweet spiced pear syrup and bright fresh lemon juice. Stirred with a few dashes of Angostura bitters for depth, it comes together quickly for a cozy evening drink.

**Yield:** 1 cocktail

## Ingredients

* 2 oz. Redemption High Rye Bourbon
* 1 oz. [Spiced Pear Simple Syrup](/recipes/spiced-pear-simple-syrup)
* 1 oz. fresh squeezed lemon juice
* 3 dashes Angostura bitters

## Directions

1. Add the bourbon, spiced pear simple syrup, lemon juice, and bitters to a mixing glass with ice.

1. Stir until well chilled and properly diluted, then strain into your glass.
