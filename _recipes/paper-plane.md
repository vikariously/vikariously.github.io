A modern classic built in four equal parts: bourbon, Aperol, Amaro Nonino, and fresh lemon juice. It's bright, bittersweet, and super easy to remember once you have the bottles on hand.

**Yield:** 1 cocktail

## Ingredients

* ¾ oz. bourbon
* ¾ oz. Aperol
* ¾ oz. Amaro Nonino Quintessentia
* ¾ oz. lemon juice, freshly squeezed
* Lemon peel (optional), for garnish

## Directions

1. Add the bourbon, Aperol, Amaro Nonino, and lemon juice into a shaker with ice and shake until well-chilled.

1. Strain into a coupe glass and garnish with a lemon peel, if using.

### Notes

* **On the rocks:** For a lazier serve, pour this into a lowball glass over rocks — just note that it dilutes fairly quickly. If you skip the shake entirely, add a splash of water to get the right dilution from the start.
