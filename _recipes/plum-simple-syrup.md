Another great use for our backyard Santa Rosa plums. This simple syrup comes together in under 30 minutes and adds a beautiful rosy color and sweet-tart plum flavor to cocktails, lemonade, or sparkling water.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup sugar
- 1 cup water
- 5-6 small plums (preferably Santa Rosa), halved and pitted

## Directions

1. Bring sugar, water, and plums to a boil in a small saucepan.

1. Remove from heat; let stand 20 minutes.

1. Strain and let cool completely.

1. Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator up to 5 days.

### Notes

* If using large plums, cut into ½-inch wedges first.
* The strained candied plums can be saved and served over ice cream.
* Can freeze to extend shelf life — the high sugar content prevents it from fully freezing, so it's easy to just dig in with a spoon to scoop out what you need.
