A bright, crunchy slaw with no mayo in sight — just olive oil, vinegar, and lime with a kick of fresh chili heat. It stays crisp and cuts right through rich BBQ, sandwiches, and mac & cheese.

![Fresh slaw alongside BBQ](/images/fresh-tangy-slaw-1.jpg)

## Ingredients

* ½ large head cabbage, quartered then shaved on mandoline (setting 3)
* ½ small red onion, quartered then shaved on mandoline (setting 2)
* 2 large fresno chilis, flesh and some seeds removed, diced into small strips (can sub jalapeños)
* ½ cup shredded carrots
* ¼ cup cilantro, diced
* 1 oz olive oil
* 1 oz white wine vinegar
* 1½ oz lime juice
* ¼ tsp salt
* Fresh ground black pepper

## Directions

1. Prep ingredients as directed.

1. Mix well and serve.

### Notes

* This amount serves 2–4 people with some leftover — the batch is sized so it doesn't get soggy.
* Making it for a party or more than 4 people? Double the recipe.
