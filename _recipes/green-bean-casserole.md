The classic holiday green bean casserole — creamy mushroom sauce and a crispy fried-onion top — upgraded with fresh green beans instead of canned. A quick blanch keeps the beans bright green with a little snap, so the whole dish tastes fresher without losing any of the nostalgia.

## Ingredients

* 2 lb fresh green beans, trimmed and cut into 1½–2" pieces
* 2 Tbsp kosher salt (for the blanching water)
* 2 (10¾ oz.) cans condensed cream of mushroom soup
* ½ cup milk
* ¼ tsp black pepper
* 1 (6 oz.) container French-fried onions (French's), about 2⅔ cups, divided

## Directions

1. **Prep the beans:** Rinse the green beans, snap or trim off the stem ends, and cut into 1½–2" pieces.

1. **Set up for blanching:** Bring a large pot of water to a rolling boil and add the salt. While it heats, fill a large bowl with ice water.

1. **Blanch:** Add the green beans to the boiling water and cook 4–5 minutes, until bright green and just crisp-tender (they'll finish cooking in the oven).

1. **Shock:** Drain the beans and immediately plunge them into the ice water to stop the cooking. Once cool (~2 minutes), drain well and pat dry with a towel — excess water will thin out the sauce.

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.

1. In a 9x13" (or ~3 qt) baking dish, combine the soup, milk, and pepper; stir until blended.

1. Stir in the green beans and half (~1⅓ cups) of the fried onions.

1. Bake for 30–35 minutes, until hot and bubbling.

1. Stir, then sprinkle the remaining fried onions over the top.

1. Bake 5 more minutes, until the onions are golden.

### Notes

* **Make ahead:** Blanch, shock, and dry the beans up to 2 days ahead and refrigerate. You can also assemble the casserole (minus the onions) a day ahead — add ~10 minutes to the first bake if it goes in cold.
* This is the original recipe doubled to fit 2 lb of fresh beans; serves ~8–10 as a side.
